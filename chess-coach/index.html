Why was Na4 played here?

15. Na4 is a positional preparation to occupy the c5 outpost. From a4, the knight provides essential overprotection for the c5 square, complementing the Be3.

This maneuver exploits Black's structural weaknesses, specifically the c5 hole and the doubled c-pawns. By establishing a dominant knight on c5, White pressures the Be6, restricts Black's queenside mobility, and cements a lasting positional advantage.
