Chess Puzzle Generator

Random mate-in-one puzzles, generated in your browser. Each side gets its king plus a random selection of its other 15 standard pieces, placed on random squares. A position is kept when it is legal and the side to move has a mate in one. While you solve, the next few puzzles are found in the background.

Settings

New settings apply from the next puzzle on. Piece counts include both kings.

Command-line version

The original generator is a Python script built on python-chess. It prints one puzzle per line as FEN, side to move, and the mating moves in SAN. 

pip install -r requirements.txt
python generate.py -n 10 --unique --side white --show
