Standing Wave & VSWR Calculator

A transmitted (incident) wave and a reflected wave travel in opposite directions along a line. Their superposition is animated below; when the two amplitudes are equal and the reflection is phase-inverted, the sum forms a true standing wave with stationary nodes. The panel also computes the reflection coefficient, the Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR), and the return loss from the two amplitudes.

VSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 − |Γ|), with |Γ| = A₂ / A₁ (reflected amplitude over transmitted amplitude).

Fixed-end reflection (e.g. a short-circuit termination) flips the reflected wave's phase by 180°, producing nodes where the sum is always zero. Free-end reflection (open-circuit termination) keeps the phase, producing antinodes at the boundary instead. In both cases, |Γ| depends only on the amplitude ratio A₂/A₁ and not on the phase convention.
