Equivalent Circuit Models of Near-Field Coupling between Electrically Small Antennas and TEM Cells

Conference paper · POSTER 2026, Prague

Abstract

Bond wires are electrically small structures that provide operating current to light sources in optical sensor packages of ICs. They inevitably form loops, emitting unwanted radiation. A fully enclosing shield for electromagnetic fields is not possible due to the need of apertures for light transmission. Transverse-electromagnetic (TEM) cells are standardized environments for characterizing such radiated emissions. This paper develops equivalent circuit models (EQCs) for the near-field coupling between electrically small antennas and TEM cells. The EQC component values are extracted from finite element method (FEM) simulations. The coupling behaviour of the EQC is compared with FEM simulations across a frequency range of 1 MHz to 3 GHz, demonstrating good agreement and confirming that the proposed models correctly represent the coupling mechanisms.
